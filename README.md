## 🚨 Problem Statement

**MSPs are bleeding clients and missing growth opportunities without knowing why.**

### Critical Business Challenges:
- 🔴 **15-20% annual client churn** with minimal early warning systems
- 💸 **Client acquisition 10x more expensive** than retention ($2,500 vs $250)
- ⏰ **Reactive management** - Problems discovered too late
- 📉 **60% service underutilization** - Clients missing value
- 📊 **Scattered insights** - Data exists but isn't actionable
- 🕐 **40% of time** account managers spend on status checks

### The Hidden Cost:
**A typical 200-client MSP loses $300K+ annually** from preventable churn and missed opportunities.

---

## 💡 Solution Overview

**NeuroNova** is an autonomous AI agent that transforms how MSPs manage client relationships by:

✅ **Predicting client churn** 60-90 days before it happens  
✅ **Identifying upselling opportunities** with personalized recommendations  
✅ **Automating relationship health** monitoring with actionable insights  
✅ **Generating retention strategies** tailored to each client's risk factors

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎯 **AI-Powered Churn Prediction**
- **84.3% accuracy** using ensemble XGBoost models
- **60-90 day early warning** system
- Multi-dimensional risk analysis:
  - Payment timeliness
  - Communication sentiment
  - Service utilization
  - Support ticket patterns
  - Contract value trends
  - Engagement frequency

### 💬 **Real-time Sentiment Analysis**
- NLP-powered communication analysis
- Tracks sentiment trends across channels
- Detects negative signals early
- Identifies relationship health indicators

### 🤖 **LLM-Powered Recommendations**
- AWS Bedrock AgentCore integration
- Context-aware action recommendations
- Success probability scoring for each action
- ROI projections
- Prioritized by impact

### 📊 **Professional Dashboard**
- **Portfolio Overview** - Health distribution, revenue at risk
- **Critical Alerts** - Clients needing immediate attention
- **Client Deep Dive** - Detailed risk factor analysis
- **Quick Actions** - Call, email, schedule with one click
- **Real-time Metrics** - Live health scores and trends

### 🔄 **Continuous Learning**
- Tracks outcome of recommendations
- Improves predictions with each interaction
- Adapts to MSP-specific patterns
- Self-optimizing models
